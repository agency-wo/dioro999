Questions

Questions before you order

How do I pay?

There is no online payment yet. You add pieces to the cart and send an order request. We confirm stock, the shipping cost and the total by email or WhatsApp, then you pay by the link we send (card or PayPal) or by bank transfer, and we ship.

Do prices include shipping?

No. Prices are in US dollars and exclude shipping. Shipping is quoted when we confirm your order, and the rates by region are listed on the How ordering works page.

Is the gold hallmarked?

Yes. Every gold piece carries a hallmark for its purity, and the listing states the purity and the weight. Bars and coins come sealed with their serial number.

Can I return a piece?

Yes. You can return an unworn piece in its original packaging within 14 days of delivery. Sealed bars and coins must stay sealed. We refund once the piece is back with us.

Do you ship outside the US?

Yes. We ship to the US, Canada, the EU and most other countries with tracking. The shipping table on the How ordering works page lists the regions, carriers and times.

Ready to choose?

Browse the shop, or send a question on WhatsApp and we answer with photos, weights and prices.
