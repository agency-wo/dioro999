About DiOro999.9

A small shop that sells gold, silver and watches online in US dollars, and says plainly what each piece is made of, how much it weighs and what it costs.

Who we are

DiOro999.9 sells gold, silver and watches online in US dollars. We are a small team and we answer questions ourselves, usually the same day.

The name carries the fineness mark of pure gold, 999.9, because that is the standard we describe everything against: every listing states the purity and the weight, and a price you can compare.

  • Every gold piece is hallmarked for its purity
  • Weights are checked on a calibrated scale before listing
  • Photos show the actual piece, not a catalogue render

How we price

Jewellery is priced per piece. The price reflects the metal, the work and any stones, and it does not move with the daily metal price.

Bars and coins follow the daily reference price of the metal: purity, weight and that reference set the figure. The price you see on the site is the latest listed one, and it is confirmed when you order.

Watches are sold at list price, new, with the maker's warranty.

We do not give investment advice. If you are buying metal as an investment, talk to a licensed adviser first.

How to reach us

WhatsApp is the fastest way. Send the name of the piece, or a photo, and we answer with the weight, the purity and the price.
